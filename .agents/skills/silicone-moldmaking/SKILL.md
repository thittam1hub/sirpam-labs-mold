# Silicone mold making

Follow `src/moldmaker/mold/SILICONE_SPEC.md` for all numbers and piece lists.

Rules:
1. Every printed box comes apart (open frame + base plate). No closed one-piece boxes.
2. Channels in the silicone are formed by printed rods (sprue cone at the highest point, vents at other high points), not only by holes in the box.
3. Two-part molds ship a parting board with key bumps and a two-stage pour order.
4. Silicone thickness 10–15 mm by default; fit gap 0.2 mm FDM / 0.1 mm resin.
5. Every piece name and notice is plain language and tells the user the assembly order.
6. Ratios and times always defer to the product data sheet.
