# Silicone tooling spec (shop-floor practice)

Sources: Smooth-On box-mold and two-piece mold guides, Formlabs and Prusa silicone-mold guides, common maker practice. Numbers are starting points; the product TDS always wins.

## General
- Silicone around the model: 10–15 mm (min 10 mm; 6–8 mm only for small soft parts).
- Box walls printed 2–3 mm+ (engine wall ratio), seams sealed with hot glue or clay.
- Box must come apart: open frame + base plate, never a one-piece closed box.
- Fit gap between printed parts: 0.2 mm FDM, 0.1 mm resin.
- Pour silicone in a thin high stream into the lowest corner; degas when possible.

## Open pour box (one part)
- Pieces: base_plate (slab + locating plug) and box_walls (open frame).
- Model glued on a printed cone stand; the cone becomes the casting pour hole after flipping.
- Demold: lift walls off, peel; cut a zig-zag release line if the part is undercut.

## Two-part block
- Pieces: base_plate, frame_bottom, frame_top (pins between frames), parting_board (model outline + key bumps), pour_rods (sprue cone at the highest point, thin vents at other high points).
- Stage 1: stack plate, bottom frame, board with model, top frame; glue rods; pour.
- Stage 2: flip, remove board, release agent (petroleum jelly or mold release) on silicone, refit plate, pour.
- Keys 5–10 mm, sprue 6–12 mm (cone wide end outside), vents 2–3 mm.

## Skin + mother mold
- Skin 3–6 mm, registration rim at the split; mother mold hugs the skin (form-fit) at 3–5 mm with flange and bolts.
- Core support: model rests on 3 printed cone feet (tip 0.6–1.2 mm) lifting it by the skin thickness, plus one top pin off the pour hole; seal the pin holes with a dab of silicone after demolding.
